# Website Structure & Information Architecture

## Navigation Hierarchy
- **HOME**: Jump to top / Hero sequence
- **ABOUT**: Biography & Academic Timeline
- **SKILLS**: Technical & Creative Stack
- **WORK**: 5 Selected Portfolio Projects
- **CREATIVE**: Visual Storytelling & Production Disciplines
- **EXPERIENCE**: Leadership & Roles (Office of Academic Affairs, ViproTech Digital)
- **CONTACT**: Direct Communication & Social Links

## Section Blueprint

### 1. Preloader (`#loader`)
- Minimalist loader with custom typography glyph, glowing progress bar, and percentage counter.
- Preloads all 240 hero frames asynchronously.

### 2. Chrome & Ambient Layers
- Fixed Top Navigation (`.chrome`) with brand mark, sound toggle (thunder atmospheric audio), and navigation links.
- Fixed Left Rail (Brand descriptor) & Right Rail (Real-time scroll percentage).
- Subtle ambient noise grain, edge vignette, Amaterasu ember hem, and synthesized lightning flash system.

### 3. Act I — Hero Sequence (`#scrub`)
- Sticky 100vh canvas container pinned across a 600vh scroll track.
- Canvas renders sequential frames: Full Portrait -> Quick Push-in -> Close-up -> Sharingan Activation -> Mangekyō Hold -> Slow Pullback -> Full Final Portrait.
- Multi-phase cinematic caption typography (`.phase`).

### 4. Act II — About & Identity (`#about`)
- Editorial narrative: Shashwat Vatsyayan's philosophy on technology and visual storytelling.
- Academic Timeline:
  - Bachelor of Engineering (CSE), Chandigarh University (2024–2028 Expected)
  - Anglo Sanskrit Model Senior Secondary School (10th: 2022, 12th: 2024)

### 5. Act III — Technical Stack (`#skills`)
- Categorized technical competencies:
  - Languages (Python, Java, C++, C, JavaScript, Swift)
  - Frontend (HTML, CSS, JavaScript, React)
  - Backend (Node.js, Express.js)
  - Database (MongoDB, MongoDB Atlas)
  - Other Tools & Frameworks (FastAPI, Docker, Vite, TypeScript, Tailwind, Web Workers, JWT, Google Gemini APIs, Git, GitHub)

### 6. Act IV — Selected Work (`#work`)
- High-impact cinematic project cards for the 5 verified projects:
  1. FBOOST AGRO (Agritech & E-commerce)

- Each entry displays: Project Index, Title, Category Badge, Real Description, Tech Tags, and Action Links (`#` placeholders).

### 7. Act V — Creative Disciplines (`#creative`)
- Showcase of filmmaking, cinematography, photography, content creation, social media, editing, branding, and digital marketing.
